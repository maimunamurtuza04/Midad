ABOUT MID'AD

Why this website exists.

Mid'ad wasn't created to sell books, it was created to share the experience of reading one.

We don't believe we sell books. We believe we share quiet moments, forgotten worlds, and stories that linger long after the final page. Every book is an invitation—to pause, to wonder, to feel, and to become someone slightly different than you were before. Some stories comfort us. Some challenge us. Others stay with us for a lifetime, finding their place in our memories long after they've left our hands. If a book reaches you through us, we hope it becomes more than a purchase. We hope it becomes a companion, a conversation, a refuge, or the beginning of something new. Because what matters isn't the book itself. It's the life you live between its pages.

A SMALL PROMISE

I will always value your time more than a commission.

If I ever recommend a book here, it's because I genuinely believe it's worth reading. If I don't think it deserves your time, it won't appear on Mid'ad.
